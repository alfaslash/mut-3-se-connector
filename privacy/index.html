Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 1, 2026 • Governing Laws: Federal Law No. 152-FZ & International Standards

This Privacy Policy explains how self-employed Artem Roshko ("we", "us", "Seller") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you visit the MUT-3 Connector website (mut3connector.com) and purchase a software license.

1. Information We Collect

We strictly limit our data collection to the minimum required for license fulfillment and customer support:

  • Email Address: Provided by you during Robokassa checkout or when contacting customer support. Used to send your order receipt, software download link, and personalized license key.
  • Hardware Identifier (HWID): An anonymized, one-way cryptographic hash of your diagnostic PC's hardware configuration (motherboard/CPU identifiers) calculated locally by the application. Under Federal Law No. 152-FZ, this hash does not constitute personal data as it cannot be used to identify a natural person.
  • Order & Transaction Identifiers: Non-sensitive transaction reference numbers generated by Robokassa to confirm payment status.

2. What We DO NOT Collect

  • No Financial Data: We do NOT collect or store your credit/debit card numbers, expiration dates, or CVV/CVC security codes. All payments are hosted directly by Robokassa.
  • No Telemetry or Vehicle Data: The MUT-3 Connector desktop application operates 100% offline. It does not monitor, collect, or transmit vehicle identification numbers (VIN), diagnostic trouble codes (DTC), or usage statistics to external servers.
  • No Third-Party Advertising Trackers: This website does not use tracking pixels, ad networks, or invasive profiling cookies.

3. Legal Grounds and Purposes of Processing

Your data is processed solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Contract execution: validating your payment and delivering the purchased digital license;
  • Technical support: assisting you with activation, setup, and compatibility queries;
  • Legal compliance: maintaining sales tax records in accordance with Russian self-employed tax regulations (NPD).

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Processors

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is shared exclusively with necessary infrastructure providers:

  • Robokassa (Payment Gateway): Receives payment transaction data necessary to process your bank card payment.
  • Email Service Provider: Used to deliver license keys and correspondence to your email address.

5. Data Retention & Security

5.1. Your email and associated HWID are retained in our secure licensing registry for the lifetime of your perpetual license to ensure ongoing support and reactivation capability.
5.2. We implement standard administrative, cryptographic, and technical safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure of your information.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to:

  • Request confirmation of what personal data we hold about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate contact information;
  • Request deletion of your contact information (note that deleting license records may prevent future key re-issuance);
  • Withdraw your consent to communication at any time.
