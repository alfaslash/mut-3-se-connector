Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective date: January 1, 2026 • Governing Law: Russian Federation

At MUT-3 Connector, we strive to deliver robust, high-performance automotive software. Because our software license is an intangible digital product delivered electronically, this Refund Policy outlines the clear rules under which refunds may be requested and granted.

1. Overview of Digital Goods

MUT-3 Connector is delivered as downloadable software paired with a customized digital License Key tied to the Customer's computer Hardware ID (HWID). Unlike physical goods, digital keys cannot be returned once activated. However, we guarantee that the software performs according to its documented specifications.

2. Conditions Under Which a Refund is Granted

A full 100% refund of the purchase price will be issued if:

  • Activation Failure: The generated License Key cannot be activated on the Customer's machine due to a confirmed bug in the licensing engine, and our support team is unable to provide a functional replacement key within five (5) business days.
  • Software Defect: The software crashes, fails to launch, or cannot apply registry profiles on a supported Windows 10 or 11 system meeting all published prerequisites, and support cannot resolve the defect within five (5) business days.
  • Substantial Description Discrepancy: The software materially fails to provide core advertised features described on the official landing page for supported genuine hardware adapters.

3. Non-Refundable Circumstances

Refund requests will be declined under the following circumstances:

  • Unsupported or Counterfeit Adapters: The Customer attempts to use an unverified low-cost cloned adapter or an adapter that lacks required 32-bit (x86) J2534 drivers, where this limitation was explicitly documented.
  • Failure to Meet System Requirements: The Customer's computer runs an unsupported operating system (e.g. Windows 7, Windows XP, macOS, Linux) or lacks required administrator permissions to write Windows registry entries.
  • Absence of MUT-III SE or VCI Driver: Failure of diagnostics caused by the Customer not having installed Mitsubishi MUT-III SE or the Bosch RNM-VI driver.
  • Vehicle-Side Wiring / ECU Issues: Communication failures caused by faulty vehicle wiring, blown vehicle fuses, flat battery voltage, or defective automotive control units.
  • Change of Mind: Simple change of mind after the license key has already been issued and successfully validated.
  • Violation of License Agreement: Decompilation, code tampering, or sharing the key with unauthorized third parties.

4. Refund Request Procedure

To request a refund, please send an email to alfaslash@gmail.com within 14 calendar days of purchase containing:

  1. Your order number / payment transaction reference from Robokassa;
  2. Your email address used at checkout;
  3. Your computer Hardware ID (HWID);
  4. A brief explanation of the problem, accompanied by a screenshot or exported diagnostic log from the MUT-3 Connector "Diagnostics" tab.

5. Inspection & Resolution Process

5.1. Upon receiving your request, our technical support will review the diagnostic log and suggest corrective steps (e.g. driver reinstall, timing adjustment) within 24 hours.
5.2. If the problem cannot be resolved and qualifies for a refund under Section 2, the refund will be approved immediately.
5.3. Upon approval, the corresponding License Key will be deactivated in our licensing database.

6. Refund Processing and Payout

6.1. Approved refunds are processed directly through the payment processor (Robokassa) back to the original payment method (bank card) used during purchase.
6.2. We do not issue cash payouts or refunds to third-party accounts.
6.3. The time required for funds to reflect in your card balance typically ranges from 3 to 10 banking days, depending on your card issuer's interbank processing timelines.
